Audio and cryptography dependencies
-----------------------------------

RNNoise noise suppression:
https://github.com/xiph/rnnoise
https://github.com/Yellow-Dog-Man/rnnoise
RNNoise uses the BSD-style license provided in its COPYING file.

RNNoise.NET / YellowDogMan.RRNoise.NET (MIT package):
https://github.com/Yellow-Dog-Man/RNNoise.Net
The Windows native rnnoise.dll is supplied by this NuGet package. DS4Windows
uses its own native interop; the package's managed wrapper is excluded from
the application references. Package identity and version are declared in
DS4Windows/DS4WinWPF.csproj and packages.lock.json in that directory.

BouncyCastle.Cryptography - The Legion of the Bouncy Castle Inc. (MIT):
https://www.bouncycastle.org/
https://github.com/bcgit/bc-csharp
Source notice: DS4Windows/Resources/BouncyCastle.NOTICE.txt.
Packaged notice: Resources/BouncyCastle.NOTICE.txt.

Other managed dependencies, including Concentus, NAudio, NLog and the WPF
libraries, are declared in DS4Windows/DS4WinWPF.csproj and its lock file.
Their upstream package metadata provides their respective license details.
