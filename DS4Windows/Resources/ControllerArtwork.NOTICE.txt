Controller artwork attribution
==============================

The controller thumbnails and DualSense remapping artwork in this directory
are adapted from Gamepad Asset Pack by AL2009man / Al. Lopez:

https://github.com/AL2009man/Gamepad-Asset-Pack

The DualSense artwork follows the adaptation used by SundayMoments/DS5_Bridge:
https://github.com/SundayMoments/DS5_Bridge

The GameCube Controller for Switch 2 illustration is a project asset created
for DS4Windows. The official Nintendo product image and control diagram were
used as visual references. The package includes the illustrated asset.

https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/nintendo-switch-2-nintendo-gamecube-controller-120833/
https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/68429/
